---
name: Product positioning
description: Activity-history-first matching, recurring events, post-event tagging, auto-formed crews — no external interest tags on profiles
type: feature
---
Core product principles:
- **Activity history > interest tags.** Users are matched on what they actually attend, not chips/tags they pick. Profiles must NOT expose outward-facing "interests" lists.
- **Recurring events drive retention.** Surface weekly/biweekly series prominently with "Nth time for you" labels. Habit formation > one-off discovery.
- **Crossed paths is a first-class signal.** Count how many times two users have co-attended; surface "familiar faces" on event cards and profiles.
- **Post-event reflection.** After attending, prompt user to tag 1–3 people they clicked with. Mutual tags unlock DMs and seed auto-formed crews.
- **Auto-formed groups.** Crews emerge from repeated co-attendance in the same series OR mutual post-event tags. No manual group creation.
- **Activity feed** showing recent events attended, who else was there, and series streaks.

Recommendations rank by: (1) series the user already attends, (2) events featuring users they keep crossing paths with, (3) category affinity from past attendance.
