Sync is built for unmarried male expats, 25-40, who moved abroad and want a consistent crew — not dates, not networking, not a tag-based social profile. Tone should be motivational, no-nonsense, slightly understated. Avoid "find your tribe / vibes / curated" marketing language. Speak to building habits, showing up, repeat sessions.
